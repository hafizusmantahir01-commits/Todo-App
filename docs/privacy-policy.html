Todo App

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

In short: Todo App does not collect any personal data. Your tasks are saved only on your own phone and never leave it.

Information we collect

None. Todo App has no account, no sign-in and no server. We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

Data stored on your device

The tasks, lists, habits, day reviews, notes, settings and focus-timer history you create are saved in the app’s private storage on your device. Only you can see them. We cannot access them.

Permissions

Todo App works offline. It never reads your location or contacts. Prayer times are calculated on your phone from the city you choose. Each permission below is asked for only when you use that feature, and you can turn it off at any time in your phone’s settings.

  • Notifications: to remind you before tasks, at prayer times, for Sehri and Iftar, and for daily and weekly planning. Reminders are created on your phone.
  • Microphone: only while you press the mic button to speak a task. Your phone’s built-in speech service turns your words into text; depending on your phone, that service may process audio online (for example Google’s speech service). Todo App does not record or store your voice.
  • Calendar (read only): only if you turn on “Show my calendar in Planner”. Events are read on your phone and shown to you. They are never changed, copied or sent anywhere.
  • Camera and photos: only if you choose to add a profile photo. The photo is saved only on your phone and is never uploaded.

Backups

If you tap “Save backup file”, the app creates a file with your data and opens your phone’s share menu. You decide where it goes (for example Google Drive or WhatsApp). The app itself does not send it anywhere.

Ads, analytics and third parties

Todo App contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking or data-sharing tools.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings › Export data copies all your tasks as text so you can keep a backup.
  • Delete: Settings › Delete all data erases everything from the app immediately.
  • Uninstall: removing the app also removes all of its data from your device.

Children’s privacy

Todo App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
